Stage 1: Foundation Setup

Asset Onboarding, Staff Registration & Service Standards

Before conducting maintenance operations, fleet units, technicians with standardized wage rates, and PM interval rules must be established in the master registries.

Primary Roles Fleet Manager, System Administrator
Source Navigation App Registry > Equipment Registry & Employee Registry
Primary Database Tables vehicles, employees, service_standards
Transaction Output Active Asset IDs, Verified Wage Rates, Baseline PM Counters

Step 1.1: Registering Fleet Equipment & Initializing PM Baselines

Navigate to /global-settings/vehicle-registry. Register each machine or transport asset.

Onyx MRO — /global-settings/vehicle-registry
Figure 1.1: Equipment Registry displaying plate numbers, model details, service standards, and initial baseline mileage setup. Click to expand
1
Click "Add Equipment"
Enter the official Plate Number (or Asset Code), Conduction Sticker Number, Vehicle Make, and Model.
2
Assign the Service Standard
Select the maintenance profile (e.g., STD-HEAVY for prime movers, STD-LIGHT for service vans). This assigns the PM2 to PM7 service interval distances.
3
Set Baseline Service Reading
Input the starting odometer reading or engine hours. Toggle Active Status to TRUE. Once saved, Onyx initializes the vehicle's PM counters.

Step 1.2: Enrolling Technicians & Wage Costing Rates

Navigate to /global-settings/employee-registry. Register mechanics, electricians, and supervisors with their financial costing rates.

Cost Accounting Rule
The Rate Per Hour represents the technician's internal wage cost to the company (used for internal Job Order cost accounting). The Sell Rate Per Hour is the billing rate charged when work is billed to external clients or dedicated cost centers.

Stage 2: Shift Operations

Daily Inspection & Telemetry Capture

The daily heartbeat of the maintenance system. At the end of every operating shift, inspectors record odometer readings and link any observed defects to FMEA condition risk codes.

Primary Roles Yard Inspector, Shift Driver, Shop Clerk
Source Navigation Maintenance Plan > Daily Inspection
Primary Database Tables vehicle_daily_logs, log_risks
System Trigger Recalculates PM Due Thresholds & Updates Master Reading
Onyx MRO — /daily-inspection
Figure 2.1: Daily Inspection Form showing equipment selector, previous reading, current reading input, daily increment, and operator remarks. Click to expand
1
Select Equipment via Vehicle Picker
Click the search icon to open the Vehicle Picker modal. Filter by plate number. Onyx automatically fetches and displays the asset's recorded Previous Reading.
2
Enter Current Service Reading
Input the vehicle's odometer or engine hour meter at shift end. Onyx dynamically calculates:
Daily Increment = Current Reading − Previous Reading
3
Record Remarks & Submit Log
Input any field observations (e.g., "Unusual air compressor cycling noticed during hill climb") and click Submit Daily Log. The vehicle's master mileage is updated instantly.
4
Optional: Attach FMEA Condition Risk Flag
If an equipment irregularity requires tracking, switch to the Inspection Flags tab, link the log entry to an Unsafe Condition Code, and attach mitigation notes.
Stage 3: Preventative Control

Equipment PM Details & One-Click Completion

Tracks PM tiers (PM2 through PM7). When accumulated service readings exceed target thresholds, red alert flags highlight the due service. Shop supervisors execute one-click completion upon physical service sign-off.

Primary Roles Maintenance Supervisor, Shop Foreman
Source Navigation Maintenance Plan > Equipment PM Details
Database Stored Procedure rpc('completed_vehicle_pm', { p_pm_level, p_vehicle_id })
Transaction Output Advances Target Counter, Records Completed Timestamp, Clears Flag
Onyx MRO — /vehicles
Figure 3.1: Equipment PM Details screen showing fleet list, current service readings, and PM schedule breakdown. Click to expand
1
Open Equipment PM Details Modal
Search by plate number and click View Details to open the PM Schedule Breakdown.
2
Review PM Schedule Grid
Examine the row for each tier (PM2 to PM7). If Current Reading >= Current PM Target, the row turns red and displays an active green Completed button.
3
Execute Completion Procedure
When physical maintenance is verified, click the Completed button. Onyx invokes the completed_vehicle_pm RPC procedure:
  • Sets pmX_previous = current_service_rdg
  • Records pmX_datecompleted = NOW()
  • Advances pmX_current = pmX_next
  • Recalculates pmX_next = pmX_current + standard_interval
  • Clears the overdue red flag immediately.
Stage 4: Forward Planning

12-Month Predictive Maintenance Forecast

Forecasts PM triggers across a rolling 12-month horizon based on historical average daily usage, enabling bay capacity planning and proactive parts requisitions.

Primary Roles Fleet Manager, Parts Planner, Maintenance Superintendent
Source Navigation Maintenance Plan > Maintenance Forecast
Data View Source vw_12monthpredictive_maintenance_details
Transaction Output Monthly bay load schedules, pre-allocated inventory requisitions
Onyx MRO — /maintenance-forecast
Figure 4.1: 12-Month Predictive Maintenance Forecast with horizon slider, PM tier filter, and monthly equipment schedule matrix. Click to expand
1
Adjust Forward Horizon Slider
Drag the slider from 1 to 12 months to customize your forward-looking planning window (e.g., set to 3 months for quarterly workshop planning).
2
Filter by PM Tier & Asset Code
Filter by PM4 or PM6 to isolate major services requiring extended bay occupancy.
3
Export Schedule for Parts Procurement
Cross-reference upcoming PM volumes with store inventory to ensure filters, synthetic oils, and brake linings are pre-ordered prior to vehicle shop intake.
Stage 5: Work Order Management

Job Order Execution, Labor Tracking & Cost Rollup

The primary operational and financial workhorse of Onyx. Creates work orders, assigns benchmarked task standards, issues bill of materials (BOM), records technician labor hours, logs external machining contracts, and rolls up total maintenance costs.

Primary Roles Shop Supervisor, Assigned Technicians, Cost Accountant
Source Navigation Job Order System > Job Order & Dashboard
Primary Database Tables job_orders, job_order_tasks, job_order_materials, job_order_resources
Financial Formula Total Cost = Labor Cost + Materials Cost + Contract Cost

Step 5.1: Job Order Dashboard & Backlog Triage

Navigate to /job-orders/dashboard to review workshop backlog, open work orders, and priority distribution.

Onyx MRO — /job-orders/dashboard
Figure 5.1: Job Order Dashboard displaying work order throughput metrics and status summaries. Click to expand

Step 5.2: Work Order Creation & Multi-Tier Costing

Navigate to /job-orders to execute the complete work order transaction lifecycle.

Onyx MRO — /job-orders
Figure 5.2: Job Order Management interface showing work order list, cost rollups, and multi-tab detail editor. Click to expand
1
Create Work Order Header
Select Equipment, check-in Odometer Reading, Priority (Urgent, High, Normal, Low), Expected Completion Date, and Assigned Supervisor. Initial status is Open.
2
Assign Task Standards
Under the Tasks tab, pick from the Task Standards library (e.g., "Brake Caliper Overhaul"). The system automatically loads preconfigured benchmark labor hours (e.g., 3.5 hrs).
3
Issue Bill of Materials (Parts)
Under the Materials tab, add stock codes, OEM part numbers, quantity, and unit price. Line totals calculate automatically and sum into the Materials Cost.
4
Assign Labor Resources & Log Hours
Under the Resources tab, select technicians from the Employee Registry and record actual charged hours. Onyx computes:
Labor Line Total = Charged Hours × Technician Hourly Wage Rate
All labor lines sum dynamically into the Labor Cost.
5
Record Subcontracted Contracts
Under Outside Contracts, log third-party repairs (e.g., crankshaft grinding). Line totals sum into Contract Cost.
6
Review Grand Total & Close Work Order
Onyx aggregates:
Total Job Order Cost = Labor Cost + Materials Cost + Contract Cost
When quality inspection is passed, set Date Completed and update status to Completed or Closed.
Stage 6: Asset Lifecycle

Component History, Teardown & Scrap Governance

Maintains serialized traceability for critical assemblies (engines, transmissions, differential carriers, hydraulic pumps). Tracks installation, removal, accumulated runtime, and marks condemned parts as scrapped.

Primary Roles Component Rebuild Specialist, Workshop Supervisor
Source Navigation Maintenance Plan > Component History
Primary Database Tables equipment_components, equipment_component_history
Transaction Output Net component run-hours, scrap audit status (is_scrapped)
Onyx MRO — /component-history
Figure 6.1: Component History screen showing serialized units, vehicle installations, accumulated mileage, and scrap status. Click to expand
1
Teardown & Component Removal
When an assembly fails or is scheduled for overhaul, locate the unit in Component History and click Remove Component. Record the vehicle reading at removal and teardown notes. Onyx computes the net running hours accumulated on that asset.
2
Scrap Evaluation (Condemnation)
If structural casing cracks or catastrophic damage prevents rebuild, flag the component as is_scrapped = TRUE. This prevents the unit from ever being remounted on any vehicle.
3
Installation of Rebuilt / New Unit
Select the target vehicle and the new/rebuilt component serial number. Click Install Component, input current vehicle mileage and installation date. Status updates to Installed = TRUE.
Stage 7: Reliability & Safety

Incident Investigation & 4-Pillar Root Cause Analysis

Closes the reliability loop when field breakdowns or safety incidents occur. Captures financial loss estimates and systematically breaks down causes across Conditions, Acts, Personal Factors, and Work-Related Causes.

Primary Roles Safety Officer, Reliability Engineer, Quality Auditor
Source Navigation Incident Mgmt > Incident Report
Taxonomy Framework 4-Pillar RCA (Conditions, Acts, Factors, Causes) + FMEA RPN
Transaction Output Corrective Actions, Incident Loss Tracking, Preventive Rule Adjustments
Onyx MRO — /incident-management/incident-report
Figure 7.1: Incident Report management screen showing incident master records, severity badges, and 4-pillar RCA tabs. Click to expand
1
Log Incident Master Record
Record Incident Reference Number, Vehicle ID, Incident Date/Time, Severity (Low, Medium, High, Critical), Reported By, and Estimated Financial Loss.
2
Conduct 4-Pillar Root Cause Analysis
Select the incident and populate findings across all 4 analysis pillars:
  1. Substandard Conditions: Mechanical failure (e.g., brake line puncture, fatigue crack).
  2. Substandard Acts: Operator behavior (e.g., driving with low air pressure warning).
  3. Personal Factors: Human factors (e.g., operator fatigue, inadequate shift handover).
  4. Work-Related Causes: Systemic causes (e.g., inadequate PM inspection checklist interval).
3
Close Loop with Preventive Actions
Use RCA findings to update PM intervals in Service Standards or adjust FMEA detection protocols in App Settings to ensure permanent mitigation.
